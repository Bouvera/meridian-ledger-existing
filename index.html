What we do

Monthly bookkeeping

We categorize every transaction, reconcile your bank and card accounts, and close the month on a set date.

Monthly reports

A profit and loss statement, a balance sheet and a short note on anything that moved, so you read numbers, not spreadsheets.

Tax preparation support

Quarterly estimate figures and a clean year-end file, handed to your CPA or ours, so filing is not a scramble.

How it works

  1. You tell us about your business on a short call. We look at your accounts and your last few months of records.
  2. We clean up anything behind, then take over the monthly work in the software you already use.
  3. Every month you get your reports by the tenth, and one person you can reach by email when you have a question.
