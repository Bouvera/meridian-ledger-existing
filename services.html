Services

Everything we do is monthly and ongoing. You pay one flat fee for your plan and we do the work.

What is included

Monthly bookkeeping. We record and categorize every transaction in your books, reconcile your bank, credit card and loan accounts, and chase down anything we cannot match. We close each month by the tenth. This is the core of every plan.

Monthly financial reports. Each close comes with a profit and loss statement, a balance sheet and a cash summary, plus a short written note on anything unusual: a jump in costs, a slow-paying customer, a missing receipt.

Tax preparation support. We calculate your quarterly estimated tax figures from the books, keep your records organized for your return, and prepare a year-end package. We work with your CPA, or we can introduce you to one of ours. We do not file returns ourselves.

Catch-up and cleanup. If your books are behind by a few months or a few years, we bring them current first, then move you to the monthly plan. Cleanup is scoped after we have seen your accounts.

Payroll and sales tax coordination. We reconcile your payroll provider and your sales tax filings against the books so the numbers agree.

To find out which plan fits, email us with a few lines about your business and we will set up a call.
